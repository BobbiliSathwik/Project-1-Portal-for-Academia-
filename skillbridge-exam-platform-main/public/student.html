SkillBridge Assessment Rules

Please read these rules before starting your assessment.

  1. Keep your face clearly visible to the camera during the assessment.
  2. Stay focused on the assessment and avoid looking away for extended periods.
  3. Do not switch to other tabs, windows, or applications during the assessment.
  4. Do not use unauthorized websites, notes, devices, or external assistance.

  6. Your webcam and microphone may be used for AI monitoring and assessment recording.
  7. Focus/eye monitoring allows up to 5 warnings.
  8. Voice/audio monitoring allows up to 5 warnings.
  9. Exceeding either warning limit may automatically terminate and submit the assessment.
  10. Complete the assessment honestly and independently.
